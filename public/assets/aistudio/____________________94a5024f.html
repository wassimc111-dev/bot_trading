Ask us
  • 01 What is SMC Signal AI Bot?

    A signal bot for binary options. You set a broker, a market, a pair and an expiry, tap Get signal, and one card answers CALL, PUT or WAIT for that candle, with an agreement meter, the entry time and the close. It is not a broker, and it never holds your money.

  • 02 What does SMC stand for?

    Smart money concepts: a way of reading a chart through liquidity, order blocks and shifts in structure. The name tells you who the bot is for. The bot itself does not mark any of those levels on your chart.

  • 03 Which brokers does it work with?

    Pocket Option, Quotex, IQ Option, Olymp Trade, Binomo, Exnova, Bubinga, Deriv and Alpari. Set yours in the Broker listbox and every signal carries its name.

  • 04 Do I have to type anything?

    No. Every answer is a listbox or a key: the broker, the market, the pair and the expiry. There is no text box and no command to remember.

  • 05 What do CALL, PUT and WAIT mean?

    CALL means the read expects the candle to close above its open, so buy up. PUT expects it below, so buy down. WAIT means trend and momentum disagree, and skipping the candle is the answer.

  • 06 What is the agreement meter?

    How firmly the three inputs behind a signal line up. A fuller meter is a cleaner read. It is not the chance the trade wins, so size every trade as if it could lose.

  • 07 Why does the card sometimes wait before the entry?

    On expiries under an hour, when less than half of the current candle is left, the bot reads the next candle and counts down to its open. Joining a candle half way is a different trade from the one that was read.

  • 08 Can I use it at the weekend?

    Yes, on OTC. The real market closes from Friday 21:00 to Sunday 21:00 UTC, so the bot keeps OTC chosen until the market reopens.

  • 09 Which expiries are free?

    4h and 1d on most brokers, 1h on IQ Option and 23h on Olymp Trade. The faster ones open with free access through a partner broker, or a $49 monthly or $199 lifetime plan.

  • 10 Does it publish a win rate?

    No. The bot never sees how your trade closed, and results change with the pair, the expiry and the market. Check each signal on your broker's chart and keep your own record.

  • 11 Is it risky?

    Yes. A short-expiry trade can lose the whole stake, and a signal is not advice. Only trade money you can afford to lose, and read the Risk warning.
